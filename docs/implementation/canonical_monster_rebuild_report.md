# Canonical Monster Rebuild Report

## 1. Summary

The old monster list was replaced with the canonical Monster Wikipedia roster from `docs/07_BLOCKMANCER_MONSTER_WIKIPEDIA_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`. The rebuilt content contains 36 regular monsters, 11 elite monsters, and 6 boss-compatible `mon_boss_*` entries to preserve the current boss loading convention. Stage pools, elite pools, boss references, friendship records, asset mappings, compatibility aliases, and content validation were updated. Build and required validators pass; missing final monster art remains fallback-safe and warning-only.

## 11. Known Limitations

- Missing final art remains the main limitation; validators warn but gameplay falls back safely.
- Some reused frames were copied from old aliases into canonical folders, so visuals may not match final monster identity until art is replaced.
- `sync:assets` still reports fallback-safe missing production assets because most final PNG/audio assets are absent.
- `audit:asset-variants` still reports unrelated hero sheet, boss intro, and boss arena warnings.
- Elite effects are aligned by behavior IDs and warnings, but several are still partial or fallback-only in runtime depth.
- Old monster IDs remain only in compatibility aliases for save/look-up safety.
